How Much Car Can I Afford? A Simple Budget Guide

By Mansoor Khan · Published November 9, 2026 · Updated November 9, 2026

The sticker price is not the real question. What matters is whether the monthly payment, insurance, fuel and repairs fit comfortably in your budget.

The 20/4/10 rule

  • 20% down payment, so you start with equity in the car.
  • 4 years (48 months) or less for the loan term.
  • 10% or less of your gross monthly income on total car costs, including insurance.

Example

If you earn $5,000 a month before tax, 10% is $500 for the payment and insurance together. With $150 insurance, aim for a payment near $350. Use the calculator to try prices until the monthly figure lands there.

Costs people forget

Sales tax, registration, insurance increases, fuel, maintenance and tires can add hundreds per month. Add them before you decide.

Get pre-approved first

Ask your bank or credit union for a pre-approved rate. You will know your real APR, and you can compare the dealer’s offer fairly.
