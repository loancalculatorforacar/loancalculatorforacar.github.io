About CarLoanCalc

Mansoor Khan builds free online finance tools and writes plain-English guides about car loans, payments and budgeting. Every example in these guides is calculated with the standard loan payment formula.

CarLoanCalc is free. The calculator runs in your browser and does not send or store what you type. Results are estimates; your lender’s final offer may differ.
